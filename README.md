# Welcome to my GitHub profile! I'm Oscar Sandgren

BSc student in Information Systems, building toward a career at the intersection of business, technology, and AI-driven decision-making.

## What I do

- Studying Information Systems with a focus on how organizations design, structure, and use data-driven systems.
- Using AI deliberately as a learning accelerator — not a shortcut. I work through problems myself first, then use AI to refine, stress-test, and go deeper.
- Building toward expertise I can actually defend: if I can't explain it or push back on it, I don't count it as learned.

## What I'm working toward

- Becoming a professional in information systems and applied AI.
- Turning technical competence into financial independence and long-term career leverage.
- A journey that is fun, challenging and rich of experience. 

## How I work

Disciplined, exact, and allergic to shortcuts that skip understanding. I'd rather be corrected early than agreed with by default.

## Let's connect

Always glad to meet people who take learning seriously — students, builders, or anyone working at the intersection of information systems and AI. If you're growing in this space too, reach out! I'd rather trade ideas and get sharper together than work in a silo.
